## Game Concept

### Core Idea

The game is an asymmetric cooperative experience designed for the *Window into VR* system.

Two players take on distinct roles:

- **VR Player (VP):** immersed in the virtual environment, able to move and interact with objects.
- **Window Player (WP):** observes the environment from an external perspective through a projection window and provides guidance.

The gameplay is based on perspective asymmetry, where each player has access to different information. Progress is only possible through communication and cooperation.



### Gameplay Mechanics

The core mechanic is an information imbalance between players:

- The VP can interact with the environment but lacks important visual information.
- The WP has access to additional or clearer information but cannot directly interact.

Key mechanics include:

- **Hidden Information:** clues and paths only visible to the WP  
- **Limited Perception:** the VP will experience darkness and distorted vision  
- **Guided Navigation:** the WP guides the VP through communication  
- **Perspective-Based Puzzles:** solutions depend on viewing angles or alignment  



### Level Design

The game consists of multiple levels with increasing complexity:

#### Level 1 – Introduction
- Simple maze navigation  
- Players learn basic communication and roles  

#### Level 2 – Puzzle Level
- Introduction of keys and locked exits  
- Hidden clues visible only to the WP  
- Requires coordination and problem-solving  

#### Level 3 – Action Level
- Introduction of dynamic threats (monster following the player)  
- The VP must react in real time based on WP instructions  

---

### Player Interaction

The experience relies entirely on **verbal communication** between players.

- The WP describes the environment and gives instructions  
- The VP executes actions based on this guidance  

Neither player can succeed alone, making cooperation essential.


### Design Goal

The goal is to create a cooperative experience in which both players are equally important despite having different abilities.

The projection window is used as an **active gameplay element**, not just a display.

## Additional Ideas (from brainstorming)
#### Idea 1 – Asymmetric Cooperation (We Were Here inspired)
- Two-player setup: VR Player (VP) and Window Player (WP)
- VP is immersed and interacts with the environment
- WP observes from outside and guides the VP
- Gameplay based on perspective asymmetry (each player sees different information)

#### Idea 2 – Blindfolded Cooperation Concept
- Inspired by “blindfolded cooking” scenarios
- VP has limited perception (e.g. cannot see certain clues or information)
- WP can see important elements but cannot interact
- Progress depends entirely on communication between both players
- Puzzle difficulty increases over time

#### Idea 3 – Advanced Mechanics & Level Ideas
- WP can see objects or hints invisible to VP
- VP may navigate in darkness while WP has enhanced vision (e.g. night vision)
- Perspective-based puzzles (e.g. parallax alignment)

**Level idea:**
- Action-oriented level where VP must avoid obstacles or threats
- Dangers are only visible to WP → real-time guidance required
